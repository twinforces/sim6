```
Dear CongressCritter,

I recently reviewed the history of the major financial crises that nearly destroyed the USA at pastperformance.grok.me, and identified the following items that should be codified into law. I think you should work on that. Prevention is worth trillions in cure, I guess.

Do not raise the insurance stamp for zombies. Deposit insurance is a backstop, not a growth plan. DIDMCA took the cap from $40,000 to $100,000 so the dead could buy brokered money.

Close insolvent books now. Do not pass a grow-out. Garn-St Germain let zombies gamble with insured deposits. The Resolution Trust Corporation was a 1990s novel because 1982 blinked.

Examiners over senators. A million dollars in donations does not buy forbearance. Five senators sat on the examiners. Lincoln grew.

Drive to the invoice. Audit standards that require a visit to the address on the invoice. Crazy Eddie passed every audit. So did Enron. So did the empty houses. Accountants know how to lie to other accountants.

A standing resolution desk. The RTC closed. TARP was a pause. Keep a desk that hunts vehicles and unwinds the wreckage in daylight, in public, the way 1989 did.

Haircuts, in writing. Split legs and no haircut is a bomb. The cubicle does not write its own haircut. Long-Term talked every desk into zero. The five investment banks asked the SEC for the same courtesy on the whole firm.

Name the put. A weekend in a room with no Treasury check is still a put. Say so out loud, or the next greed will assume the backstop is free.

Keep the wall. Commercial deposits stay off the proprietary book. GLBA ratified a screen door because 1998 worked. The houses still found another costume.

Auditor independence. The firm that designs the books cannot also sign them. Andersen was the consultant and the auditor. Arthur Levitt tried. Congress watered it.

Sarbanes-Oxley is not done. The CEO signed. The next paper company had a new name. Hunt the off-balance-sheet vehicle. An SIV is an SPE in a new costume.

Originators keep the risk. Make the loan, keep a piece. Originate-to-sell is how the cubicle never had to drive to the house.

Do not restamp leftover as AAA. The unsold slice of a pile of weak loans is still a pile of weak loans. The ratings firms get paid by the people selling the product. They do not get a second try.

Jail is not only for cons. Keating went to jail. Skilling went to jail. Designing a factory of weak loans stamped AAA is not investing. Investing is risk. A carnival is a crime. So is the factory.

Those who do not learn from history are doomed to repeat it, especially if they do not write their congressman.

Sincerely,
Your Name
```
