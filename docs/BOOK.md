# Book, greed, grift

Collaboration table. Do not dump this into the player HUD.

The Street noun **book** is real: the pile of positions. Accounting **books** (plural) are real: the file the auditor signs. The overlay stole the Street noun and made it mean "the game continues," and later cards used **the next book** to mean "the next scheme." That is the muddle.

## Recommendation

| Sense | Keep? | Word |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Pile of positions on a desk | Yes | **the book** (Street noun, HUD bar) |
| The file an auditor signs | Yes | **the books** (plural) |
| Overlay after a hindsight click | Change | **You have found a victory point. The greed continues.** |
| The next nested scheme | Often | **the next greed** / **the next pile**. Grift only when there is a con. |
| Marking a gain | Yes | **booking** the gain (accounting verb) |
| "Not our charter" | Change | **not our problem** / **the next guy's problem** |

**Greed vs grift.** The starting theme is each round of excessive greed licensing the next round of greed. RTC worked, so Greenwich got bigger. Greenwich worked, so Enron's auditor signed. Enron died, so we called accounting fixed, and the houses used the costume. That chain is greed, not always a con.

**Grift** is the carnival: Crazy Eddie, Enron's paper companies, leftover stamped AAA. A duration gap is not a grift. Volcker did not con anyone. TARP is a pause, not a con. If we stamp grift on every pile we lie.

**Book** stays when we mean the positions. HUD: "The book." Seizure: "The book seized you." Housing book, energy book, cut the book: those are the pile.

Applied 2026-09-20. Overlay was already greed. Street noun retags that were Proposed (not Keep) are in. "Keep, or" rows stayed Keep. HUD bar is still **the book**.
